Introducing the Clinical Reasoning Engine: an autonomous multi-agent system transforming unstructured hospital discharge notes into structured clinical intelligence.

Hospital discharge summaries are narrative, multi-author, and written under severe time pressure — resulting in latent contradictions, omitted secondary diagnoses, and unaddressed admission symptoms.

The Clinical Reasoning Engine solves this with:
• Multimodal Ingestion: Scanned PDF OCR (PyMuPDF + Tesseract), clinician audio dictation (Whisper STT with human approval), and edge language translation (MarianMT across DE/FR/NL/ES).
• LangGraph State Graph: 5 decoupled reasoning agents (Timeline Segmenter, Differential Agent, Contradiction Agent, Confidence Agent, Meta Agent) with intermediate policy checkpoints.
• Hybrid Relational & Vector RAG: SQLite/Neon PostgreSQL engine parity with 1024-dimension pgvector embeddings for rapid clinical guideline retrieval.
• Calibrated Confidence Scoring: 8 perturbed inference passes computing prediction variance and logistic regression calibration.
• 1,000-Case Empirical Benchmark: 94.2% Top-1 Diagnostic Accuracy, 100.0% Top-3 Accuracy, 0.9705 MRR, and 54.9 cases/sec throughput across 1,000 synthetic patient records.

Deployed on Google Cloud Run serverless microservices with React audit UI.
